Our Story

About Agro AI

Built in Addis Ababa for farmers across Ethiopia and Oromia — bringing AI to the fields that feed the country.

Our Mission

To empower every Ethiopian farmer — from smallholders in Oromia to cooperatives across the country — with AI tools that detect crop diseases early, recommend the best crops for local soil and climate, and provide real-time weather and market insights, so they can grow more, waste less, and earn better.

Our Vision

An Ethiopia where every farm — from the teff fields of Oromia to the enset gardens of the south — is a smart farm, where data and technology work hand-in-hand with the farmer's knowledge to build a food-secure, sustainable future for generations to come.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

Farmer First

Every decision we make starts with one question: does this make the farmer's life better? Simplicity, clarity, and usefulness are non-negotiable.

Science-Backed

Our recommendations are grounded in peer-reviewed agricultural research, real-world data, and validated AI models — not guesswork.

Inclusive Access

Great technology should be accessible to all. We build Agro AI to work on any device, in any language, for any farmer anywhere in the world.

Sustainability

We promote farming practices that protect the environment, restore soil health, and ensure that future generations inherit a healthy planet.

Trustworthiness

Farmers trust us with their livelihoods. We take that seriously — being transparent about our model confidence levels, data sources, and limitations.

Continuous Innovation

Agriculture evolves. So do we. We are committed to continuously improving our AI models, adding new features, and responding to farmers' changing needs.

Our Journey

Agro AI Timeline

  1. 2020

    The Idea Is Born

    A team of agronomists, AI researchers, and software engineers in Addis Ababa come together with a shared vision: use AI to solve the crop disease problem that costs Ethiopian farmers billions of birr every year.

  2. 2021

    First AI Model Trained

    Our first disease detection model is trained on labelled crop images spanning teff, enset, coffee, maize, and other crops grown across Oromia and the Ethiopian highlands. Early accuracy results exceeded 90%, validating the core concept.

  3. 2022

    Beta Launch & Field Testing

    Agro AI enters public beta. Over 10,000 farmers across Oromia and other regions of Ethiopia use the platform during field trials, providing invaluable real-world feedback.

  4. 2023

    Weather & Crop Recommendation Launched

    Live weather integration (powered by Open-Meteo) and the personalised Crop Recommendation engine are added, making Agro AI a full farm intelligence platform.

  5. 2024

    Agro AI v2.0 — Full Suite

    Full redesign with dark mode and a Learning Center with 100+ articles tailored to Ethiopian farming. The platform now serves tens of thousands of active farmers across Ethiopia.
